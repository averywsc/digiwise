Real or scam

Messages drop into your inbox. Block the scams, keep the real ones.

Spot the four tells

  • PressureA deadline or countdown.
  • Odd linkExtra words, dashes, a zero for an o.
  • It asksA password, a code, card details or money first.
  • Too good or scaryFree prizes, refunds, accounts about to vanish.

Two or more tells is almost always a scam.
